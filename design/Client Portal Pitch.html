02 — Discovery

What we heard.

01

Repair intake is the biggest gap.

Today it's a handwritten envelope. With a work cell, intake becomes: photograph the watch, type the details, print the envelope, customer logged.

02

Customers can't be reached easily.

Landlines can't text. Voicemails don't get returned. A simple "your watch is ready" SMS would cut pickup time and missed calls dramatically.

03

Don't replace what works.

TurboTenant and Square stay. The dashboard pulls them together and fills the holes around them — customer records, intake, expenses by property.

Snap a photo. Log the repair. Done.

Three automations in phase one.

11 — Virginia compliance

Where state law touches the build.

VRLTA
Rent, deposits, late fees, notices live under the Virginia Residential Landlord & Tenant Act.Because TurboTenant is the system of record, they handle the compliance machinery — we don't take that liability on.
Notices
Dashboard reminders are courtesy only — never legal notices.Formal notices (5-day pay-or-quit, lease termination) flow through TurboTenant or your attorney, exactly as today.
Payments
No payment processing in the dashboard. Ever.Reading balance + status is fine; touching money would put us inside payment-processor and landlord regulations we'd rather avoid.
Data
Tenant and customer data stays inside the source platforms.The dashboard caches what it needs to render the screen and nothing more — minimum data, encrypted at rest.
